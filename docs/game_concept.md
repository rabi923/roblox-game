# Hotel Hermes — Game Concept Document

## Overview
**Hotel Hermes** is a multiplayer roguelite survival horror experience built on Roblox. Players check into an eerie, 1920s Art Deco grand hotel where an elevator serves as the sole pathway upwards through procedurally generated floors.

---

## Core Gameplay Loop
1. **The Lobby**:
   - Players spawn into the grand vintage lobby with checkered marble floors and art deco architecture.
   - Interact with the **Reception Desk** to Check In (First run is free, subsequent runs 5 Robux or daily ticket).
   - Spin the **Room Service Wheel** once every 24 hours for bonus Hotel Coins, extra lives, or items.
   - Enter the **Ornate Brass Elevator** to ascend to Floor 1.

2. **Floor Progression**:
   - Each floor features procedural corridor labyrinths (`Hallway_Straight`, `Hallway_LTurn`, `Hallway_TJunction`, `Hallway_DeadEnd`).
   - Rooms contain interactive furniture and search spots (`GuestRoom_Standard`, `Bathroom_Standard`, `Kitchen_Service`, `SecretRoom_Library`).
   - Solve floor puzzle objectives (colored keycards, electrical fuses, safe code notes, ritual candles) to re-power the elevator.
   - Avoid or distract wandering entities using throwable wine bottles or ceramic mugs.
   - Hide under elevated beds or inside gothic wardrobes to escape chase sequences.

3. **The Push-Your-Luck Decision**:
   - Reaching the elevator on a cleared floor presents players with a crucial choice:
     - **CONTINUE ▲**: Ascend to higher, harder floors with steeper rewards. If caught, all unsaved loot from this run is lost!
     - **CHECK OUT 🏨**: Return to the lobby, permanently banking all gathered Hotel Coins and lore items to your profile.

---

## Monetization Design (Ethical & High Conversion)
* **Check-In Ticket**: 5 Robux for run entry (micro-transaction frictionless price point).
* **VIP Elevator Express**: Skip directly to previously cleared milestone floors.
* **Revive / Second Chance**: 15 Robux to resurrect inside the elevator cab once per run.
* **Cosmetic Flashlight & Suit Skins**: Custom vintage art deco flashlight beams and period outfits.
